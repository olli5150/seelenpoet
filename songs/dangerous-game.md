# Dangerous Game — SeelenPoet feat. SinDee

## Suno Style Prompt

Dark melodic German rap, cinematic trap, gritty alternative rock. 104 BPM, F# minor. Driving punchy beat, hard kick, crisp snare, deep controlled 808 bass, syncopated hi-hats with subtle swing. Infectious groove, tight bass-drum interplay, powerful forward momentum. Beautiful solo violin intro, then heavy beat drop. Palm-muted electric guitar riffs, atmospheric synths, memorable violin melodies. Verses: F#m(add9)–F#m(add9)–Dmaj7–C#7. Chorus: F#m(add9)–Dmaj7–Bm(add9)–C#7(b9). Charismatic male storytelling rap with melodic touches. Biting female melodic rap, short lines, sharp accents. Short male-female duet hook, dark catchy melody, rhythmic unison, raw grit, clipped endings. Powerful yet melodic, clear upfront vocals, dynamic builds, explosive final hook with soaring violin counter-melody. No Schlager, sugary pop harmonies or sentimental ballad vocals.
